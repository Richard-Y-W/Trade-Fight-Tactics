  WELCOME TO THE FLOOR
Build an edge. Watch it trade.

  
Start with 10 recruitment credits. Recruits join your eight-slot bench. In the fast beta, deploy 3 on Monday, 4 Tuesday, 5 Wednesday, 6 Thursday, and 7 Friday. Only deployed units supply trading capabilities and traits.

  
Every company draws from the same limited supply. Each identity has 24 / 20 / 16 / 12 / 9 copies by cost tier. Offers reserve copies; selling or elimination returns them. Scout the rival’s floor and bench in the right sidebar.

  
Units have a style and a specialty. Styles activate at 2 / 4 distinct identities; specialties at 2 / 3. Open “Active benefits & next thresholds” for effects and costs. Three identical units of the same star merge, up to three stars.

  
Career tiers are Entry, Mid, Lead, PM, and Firm Owner (provisional). Grey, green, blue, purple, and gold show rarity. Merging increases stars, not career tier.

  
Daily shop odds appear above five offers. Reroll costs 2 credits; lock holds remaining offers through the next day. Gain 4 base credits each new day, plus 1 interest per 10 saved credits (maximum 5). Interest uses your closing balance before income.

  
Green is your firm. Blue is the rival. A 40-second day shares customer flow across Equities on Monday; Options Tuesday; Rates and FX Wednesday; Commodities Thursday; Credit Friday. Your build unlocks controls. Stance changes spread and size; other buttons need the right capability. Pausing a market stops new quotes, but keeps existing holdings exposed.

  
Inventory pressure measures position-limit usage. Estimated one-second sigma measures price risk under the current game model, not maximum loss. Gross exposure counts absolute holdings.

  
Net marked-to-market P&L after costs wins. Positions settle daily. The right rail contains local bots and background pairings. Clicking a bot inspects its actual roster; live spectating comes later.

  
Weekend options cost $8 from trading capital and settle next Monday. Options are not marked intraday. Recruitment credits are separate from trading cash.
