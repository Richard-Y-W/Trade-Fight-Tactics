# Unit behavior matrix

Existing pool IDs and 32-unit roster retained. The following identities are the featured behavior set; other units deliberately retain family policies and differ in grade/traits. Careers, stars and behavior remain separate.

| Existing ID / display name | Decision and information | Execution / exposure | Visible evidence / weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| maker / Junior Market Maker | Delivered belief, inventory skew | Two-sided passive offers, ordinary refresh | Two monitors, quote tickets, own net and fills; stale quotes and inventory can lose |
| execution / Low-Latency PM | Same observation rules as teammates | Reprice every tick, earlier simultaneous arrival; cannot jump older FIFO | Server-like multi-monitor station, rapid tickets; cancellation loses priority |
| manual / Junior Macro Trader | Player conviction and Press | Manual window of IOC orders, otherwise quotes | Lever/arrow station; empty liquidity or wrong tilt wastes opportunity |
| broker / Momentum Trader | Last four public moves | Slower offers, directional target up to 12 units | Directional orders, large holding swings; reversals punish |
| value-clerk / Mean Reversion Apprentice | Last twelve public marks' mean | Opposes deviations, target eight units | Patient offers; sustained trends can keep moving against it |
| analyst / Signal Analyst | Noisy hidden value observed one tick late | Research-only; delivered weighted belief supports quoting | Purple support pulse, next-move accuracy proxy; no own fabricated profit |
| research / Microstructure Researcher | Noisy current hidden value, no future samples | Faster observation than Entry research, shared diminishing-return noise | Research antenna; wrong assessed direction flashes stress |
| clerk / Risk Analyst | Actual shared inventory | Stops new position-increasing offers above 18 units; emergency trim is paid | Guard events / blocked-quote count; old orders may still fill; existing positions remain exposed |
| hedge / Portfolio Risk Lead | Actual shared inventory, cost/threshold | Paid position liquidation beyond buffered threshold | Shield links to actual closed owners; quantity and cost, never a fake prevented-loss value |
| agency-lead / Relative Value Lead | Normalized NOVA/VOLT price gap from public marks | Two passive legs with opposite target exposure, independent fills | Paired market links; incomplete fills and relationship breakdown can lose |
| specialist / Volatility PM | Opening strike and final NOVA mark | Long straddle, quantity 3×grade, total premium $1.60×quantity; no quoting/Press unlock | V-shaped payoff station; small move loses premium, expiry settles once |

Relative value is an experimental fixed-ratio relationship, not a fitted cointegration strategy or atomic arbitrage. The Volatility PM is expiry-only: no Greeks, volatility surface or intraday option value. Risk Analyst is a guard, while Portfolio Risk Lead liquidates existing inventory. Protections are costs and constraints, not damage shields.

Research observations are combined by squared-grade weights. Each quoting event records delivered support sources; the renderer pulses from a researcher only when that information is actually used. Signal evaluation compares the delivered direction with the next public-mark move. This is a measured proxy and does not establish causal usefulness.

Most policies operate across all open markets. Relative Value Lead is limited to NOVA/VOLT; Volatility PM uses NOVA. Spatial adjacency is not a financial mechanism in this pass. No equipment claims or inactive item controls are added.
